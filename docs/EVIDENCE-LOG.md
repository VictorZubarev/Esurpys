# Esurpys Evidence Log

## Purpose

This document records verifiable development milestones, individual contributions, product results, research activities, and independent recognition related to Esurpys.

It supports accurate project documentation and future professional or immigration-related evidence review.

## Project Information

- Project: Esurpys
- Repository: https://github.com/VictorZubarev/Esurpys
- Current stage: Early development

## Development Milestones

For each milestone, record:

- Date
- Description
- Individual contribution
- Related commit or release
- Supporting evidence
- Verification method
- Outcome

## Product Development

Record completed features, technical improvements, testing results, releases, and known limitations.

## Usage and Impact

Where reliable data becomes available, record:

- Verified users and usage metrics
- Customer feedback
- Revenue or commercial activity
- Independent evaluations
- Documented product outcomes

Include the measurement period, source, methodology, and limitations for each metric.

## Research and Publications

Record research questions, methodology, individual contributions, findings, publication status, and original publication links.

Do not describe planned or submitted work as published research.

## Media and Recognition

Record independently verifiable media coverage, awards, judging invitations, expert assessments, and other relevant recognition.

Preserve original sources, dates, and supporting documentation.

## Evidence Integrity

- Distinguish plans from completed achievements.
- Preserve original records and source links.
- Attribute contributions accurately.
- Do not invent metrics, awards, publications, or endorsements.
- Do not publish confidential information or sensitive personal data.

## Immigration Context

The relevance of each item to EB-1A or EB-2 NIW must be evaluated against the applicable legal requirements. A repository or individual achievement does not automatically establish eligibility.
