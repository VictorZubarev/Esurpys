# Esurpys: Potential Impact in the United States

## 1. Purpose

This document describes the potential value of Esurpys in the United States, with a focus on helping people explore visa options and international destinations through a test-based Visa Navigator.

It is a forward-looking planning document. It does not claim that the project has already achieved measurable economic, social, or technological impact.

## 2. Problem Area

People considering travel, study, work, or relocation to another country may find it difficult to navigate the many visa categories, eligibility criteria, and official information sources involved.

Information can be distributed across multiple government websites, and the relevance of a particular visa category depends on individual circumstances and current legal requirements.

Esurpys aims to make the initial exploration of visa options more accessible through a guided test and a structured web interface.

The platform is intended to support preliminary exploration, not replace official immigration resources, legal advice, or formal eligibility assessments.

## 3. Potential Benefits

Potential areas of benefit include:

- **Information accessibility:** helping users begin exploring visa categories and international destinations through a guided experience.
- **Simplified exploration:** organizing an initial discovery process around users' answers and goals.
- **User experience:** presenting questions, navigation, and results in a clear and accessible format.
- **Informed next steps:** encouraging users to identify relevant official sources and verify current requirements.
- **Digital technology development:** demonstrating practical software engineering through the development of a multilingual web application.

These benefits are objectives to investigate, not established outcomes. Their actual value will depend on implementation quality, information reliability, and user evaluation.

## 4. Potential Relevance to the United States

The United States may be relevant to Esurpys users who want to explore available U.S. visa categories and identify official resources for further research.

If the platform provides appropriate U.S.-related content and is validated through suitable testing, it could help users begin navigating publicly available immigration information.

Potential relevance may be evaluated through evidence such as:

- Documented U.S.-related features and their scope.
- Reviews of the clarity and presentation of visa-related information.
- Usability evaluations involving clearly defined tasks.
- Measured task-completion rates and user-reported difficulties.
- Verified user feedback and documented case studies.
- Evidence that users can identify relevant official government resources.

Any claims about improved immigration outcomes, economic growth, job creation, or broader national benefits should be made only when supported by appropriate evidence.

## 5. Evaluation Plan

As the platform develops, its potential impact may be evaluated through the following steps:

1. Define specific user needs related to exploring visa options.
2. Establish measurable evaluation criteria.
3. Test whether users can understand the questions and navigate the available results.
4. Assess the clarity and perceived usefulness of the information presented.
5. Review whether relevant official sources are clearly identified.
6. Document the evaluation methods, participant numbers, results, and limitations.
7. Use the findings to guide improvements to the platform.

Usability and user satisfaction alone do not establish the legal accuracy of visa-related results or confirm that a user qualifies for a particular visa.

## 6. Evidence and Documentation

Relevant records may include:

- Version-controlled source code and development history.
- Technical documentation and documented product changes.
- Test results and usability evaluation reports.
- Verified user feedback, collected appropriately.
- Documentation of relevant official information sources.
- Verifiable collaborations, presentations, or publications, if they occur.

Evidence should be accurate, dated where appropriate, and consistent with the underlying records. Personal and sensitive immigration-related information should be protected.

## 7. Limitations and Responsible Use

Esurpys is an informational exploration tool under development.

Visa categories, eligibility criteria, application procedures, and government policies may change. Users should verify current requirements through official U.S. government sources, including U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services and the U.S. Department of State, as appropriate.

The platform should not present preliminary suggestions as guarantees of eligibility, visa approval, or successful immigration outcomes.
